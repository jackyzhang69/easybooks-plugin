# EasyBooks — first session orientation

EasyBooks helps self-employed Canadians keep their books through plain conversation instead of spreadsheets or direct backend access. You record income and expenses, attach receipts, create and send invoices, and scan Gmail for purchase receipts. EasyBooks does not invent amounts or dates, and it does not write to your books without going through the product. On a first session, try recording one expense you already know, such as a recent software subscription or office supply purchase. The agent may ask you to provide missing facts in plain language, such as the amount, date, or whether a purchase was business or personal. It may ask you to confirm parsed rows before they are recorded, especially for file imports and invoices. Before sending an invoice to a client, it may ask you to confirm the recipient and totals. Machine work such as sign-in and install repair stays silent unless something truly needs your judgment. You stay in control of what gets written into your books.
