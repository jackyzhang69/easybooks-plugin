# Get started (EasyBooks)

EasyBooks helps self-employed Canadians keep their books without touching spreadsheets or the backend directly. You record income and expenses, attach receipts, create and send invoices, and scan Gmail for purchase receipts. Categories, clients, and invoices stay in your EasyBooks account while the agent works through one bundled product. A natural first step is to post a receipt or expense, or to inspect a recent transaction you already logged. The agent may ask you for factual details such as the amount, date, vendor, or whether a purchase was business or personal. Before recording a batch import or sending an invoice by email, you will be asked to confirm the resolved rows. When a client or category name is ambiguous, you may be asked to verify which one you meant. Before sending feedback to Jacky, you will be asked to confirm the draft. The agent handles connect, parsing, and validation silently; you never need to run diagnostics yourself. Results are explained in plain language with the amounts and actions taken.
